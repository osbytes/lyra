A new look and layout throughout, from the Lyra interface system. Lyra opens on Home, a dashboard of the week: sessions and recorded time against the week before, recorded time per day, what needs you, recent sessions and tags. A meeting reads as panes side by side: the transcript, with sticky section headings and a menu of its sections, and your notes above the speakers and the summary. The speakers share one timeline you can hover, click or right-click to play, jump, step through someone's turns or name them. Press play on any line to play on from there, with a player bar to pause and seek. Record, Record more and Stop sit in the session's header, and a work rail under the title shows recording, quiet levels for the mic and computer audio, and processing. Adding a line or section to your notes puts a compact reference there that jumps back to it, and every jump outlines where you landed. ⌘K also finds Preferences pages, quick toggles, microphones and saved voices. Activity moves to a status bar at the bottom. Narrow windows get the library as a drawer, and down to 320 px a single column with tabs. The details panel and the floating recording dock are gone, and summaries are no longer edited by hand. Also new: IBM Plex Sans and Literata, a high-contrast theme, speaker badges that differ by shape as well as colour, and a compact density in Preferences.
