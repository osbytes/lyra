'lyra': patch
---

Sturdier recordings and sessions. A crash, a failed or interrupted model download, or opening your notes with an older or newer version of Lyra no longer loses a recording or damages a session. Saved voices no longer learn from Lyra's own guesses, so one wrong match can't make the next one more likely. The meeting prompt and starting and stopping a recording are more reliable, and this release includes security hardening throughout. Intel Macs now get their own download.
