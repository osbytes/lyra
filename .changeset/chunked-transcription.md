'lyra': minor

Recordings are now transcribed a stretch at a time with Whisper large-v3-turbo (a one-time ~575 MB download) instead of streaming draft text that rewrites itself. Lines appear finished, a few seconds after each pause, and the transcript shows that Lyra is listening or catching up instead of half-heard words. On a call, your microphone and the computer's audio are transcribed separately, so your lines are labeled as you and the other side as Remote while you record, and the other side playing through your speakers no longer shows up as you. The dock shows a level meter for each, and warns when your microphone isn't picking anything up. Speakers are still settled from the whole recording when you stop.
