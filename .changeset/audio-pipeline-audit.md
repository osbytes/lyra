'lyra': minor

Clearer transcripts in rooms and on calls. Lyra now tells speech from music, fans and typing with a speech-detection model, removes your speakers' sound from the microphone with an echo canceller, and records the microphone without the browser's processing, which cost accuracy. Settings → Recording has a transcription language (your system language by default, or detect automatically), and the filter for phrases Whisper invents in silence now works in every language. A Bluetooth headset in call mode shows a notice, and computer audio reconnects when the output device changes.
