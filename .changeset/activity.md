Long work now stays findable wherever you are. Model downloads, summaries, speaker labeling, bulk summary rewrites and updates show in an Activity list at the foot of the sidebar, or behind a button in the top bar when the sidebar is hidden. A finished task you weren't looking at gets one toast, and a failure stays, with Retry, until you deal with it. Closing Settings no longer hides a download or loses a bulk rewrite's result, and a session whose summary failed is marked in the list.
