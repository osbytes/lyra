'lyra': minor

Lyra now offers to record when a call starts: in Zoom, Teams, Slack, Discord or another call app, or in Google Meet and other meeting sites in your browser. A small card asks whether to record, and Record captures the call's audio as well as your mic. It works while Lyra is closed to the tray, and "Open at login" now starts Lyra there. It checks on this computer only. Turn it off in Settings → Prompts, where you can also add apps Lyra shouldn't ask about, and import or export that list.

On Windows, "Offer a session when the mic turns on" works again on recent Windows 11 builds, which stopped reporting mic use the way Lyra used to read it.
