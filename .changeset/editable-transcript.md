Meeting transcripts are now editable line by line, with speakers in the margin and a confidence signal for lines that may be someone else. Click a speaker to confirm Lyra's guess in one click, pick from saved voices ranked by match, fix a single line, listen to a clip, or split a speaker who was really two people (with Undo). Generated meeting notes sit in their own section, so rewriting them never touches your notes.

**Upgrade:** dictation now records audio too, kept on this computer for as long as Settings → Meetings → Keep recordings says (30 days by default). Meetings from earlier versions are converted on first launch, and their kept audio moves into the session.
