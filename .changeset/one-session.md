Lyra now has one kind of session. "Continue session" in the dock adds to the open session, and New session (Ctrl+N, the + button or the global shortcut) starts one and begins recording. The dock shows what is being recorded (your mic, plus computer audio) and lets you change it in place. Speakers are labeled automatically when more than one voice is heard; a solo session stays plain notes. Sessions can be tagged, the sidebar filters by tag, and a details side panel (Ctrl+. or the panel button) shows dates, duration, tags, speakers and actions. Every dropdown is now a Lyra menu instead of the system one.

**Upgrade:** the separate meeting shortcut and the tray's "Start meeting" item are gone; the one shortcut (Ctrl+Shift+Space by default) starts a session. Whether the computer's audio is recorded now follows Settings → Meetings → Capture computer audio for every session.
