New "Larger speaker model" setting (Recording): a larger model works out who spoke when, while you record and once the session ends, and the names you see while recording no longer change afterwards. In meeting rooms with several people it finds the right number of speakers far more often and gives about half as many words to the wrong person. Off by default; turning it on downloads 121 MB. Not available on Intel Macs.
