A long meeting's sections now sit beside the transcript as a table of contents that stays in view and jumps straight to each topic, folding into the transcript bar when the window is narrow. The summary folds to its first lines and a count of decisions, action items and questions, and Rewrite lives on the summary itself. Summaries call people by name instead of guessing pronouns. The title stays at the top while you scroll, and is suggested again after a summary unless you named the session yourself. Sessions show when they started apart from when they were last edited, and the sidebar lists them by date created, with a button to sort by last updated instead.
