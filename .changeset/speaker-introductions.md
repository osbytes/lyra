With summaries on, the notes model now works out who the speakers are from what was said ("Hi, I'm Maya", or being spoken to by name) before it writes the summary, so the summary has their names. Each is a guess the transcript asks you to confirm: "Is this Maya?", with the line that names them to play. Someone already in People is matched by name; anyone new is saved, with their voice, only once you say yes. A speaker you named, or a saved voice Lyra is sure of, is never renamed this way.
