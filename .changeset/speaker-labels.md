Meeting transcripts now say who spoke. After a meeting, Lyra identifies the speakers on your computer, names them from saved voices, and re-transcribes each turn. Record a voice sample in Settings > People, or name a speaker from the bar on a meeting note to relabel every line. Saved voices are recognized more reliably in later meetings.

**Upgrade:** speaker detection downloads about 32 MB of models the first time you finish a meeting.
