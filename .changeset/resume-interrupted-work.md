Work a quit or crash cuts short now picks up where it stopped the next time Lyra starts. A recording's words are saved every few seconds, and any audio that wasn't transcribed yet is transcribed from the saved recording. Speakers are then settled and the summary written, as they would have been. Long steps keep what they had already done: a diarized recording isn't diarized again, and a long meeting's summary only asks the model for the parts it hadn't answered yet. Downloads already resumed from where they stopped.
