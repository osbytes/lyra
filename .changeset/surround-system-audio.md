---
'lyra': patch
---

Calls are recorded on Windows 11 even when your headset or speakers are set to surround sound (7.1), where Lyra used to record only your microphone. Computer audio is now taken from every app but Lyra, on whichever device it plays. When computer audio can't be recorded, Lyra tries again and says so with a Retry button, and the speaker meter dims, instead of quietly recording your microphone only.
