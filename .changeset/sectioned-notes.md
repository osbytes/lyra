Long meetings get notes by section: the transcript is cut where the topic changes, each section is summarized with a recap of the ones before it, and the summary lists the sections with their start times. Action items get a pass of their own, so the on-device model finds them instead of leaving the list empty, and items from every section are kept without repeats. Each session now records how its notes were made, and Settings → Summaries offers to rewrite notes made by an earlier version (never ones you edited) or to write them for meetings that have none.
