Speakers are now identified live while you record. Each phrase becomes a transcript line for the person who said it, and the transcript grows at the end of the page. With one voice it reads as plain paragraphs; speaker names appear in the margin once a second person talks. Nothing on the page moves when someone new speaks.

**Upgrade:** There is no longer a "Transcribing / identifying speakers" step after you stop recording, and new recordings no longer write into the notes field: notes are only what you type. Existing sessions are unchanged.
