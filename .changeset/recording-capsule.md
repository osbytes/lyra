Recording reads the same in the window and in the floating pill. While you record, the session's Record button becomes a soft red capsule holding Stop, the time and one meter; the pill is that capsule on its own, smaller, and opens Lyra when clicked. Stop's ring grows with your voice in place of a pulsing dot and a "Listening" label. The mic and computer audio share the meter: your mic in grey, the call drawn over it in teal, so you can see who's talking. The meter no longer turns amber on ordinary loud speech.
