Drag the edges of the sidebar and the details panel to size them, up to a maximum that leaves the page room; Lyra remembers the widths. Drag either one past its narrowest width to close it, or drag the edge back out from the window's side to reopen it. The sidebar also has a button in the top bar and Ctrl/Cmd+\ to show and hide it. The transcript bar snaps the same way: drag it close to the top or bottom to close the notes or the transcript.
